From Electron to Proton ⚛️

You brought a different kind of energy into my life. You made me feel a little more grounded, a little more understood, and a little less alone. Thank you for being part of my journey.

My Safe Place 🫂

Sometimes, the world gets too loud, and my own thoughts become too much. But there's something comforting about knowing that someone like you exists in my life. You became a safe place for me, not because you had all the answers, but because being understood matters. Thank you for making ordinary moments feel a little less heavy.

Some memories look small to everyone else, but they mean the world to us. The random conversations, silly jokes, unexpected laughs, and little moments I probably never thanked you for... They all have a special place in my heart. Here are a few memories I want to keep close. 💗

Thank You for Being You 🧸

Thank you for your kindness. Thank you for your time. Thank you for listening to my nonsense. Thank you for all the laughs. Thank you for simply being yourself. I don't expect you to be perfect, and I hope you never feel you have to be. The person you are is already someone worth appreciating.

For the Days Ahead 🌈

But I hope life gives you countless reasons to smile, people who treat your heart gently, and courage for every difficult day. I hope you achieve the things you dream of and never forget how much you matter. You deserve happiness, Dora. Always. 💗

One More Memory 🌻

If someone asked me what makes a friendship special, I'd say it's not just the big moments. It's having someone with whom even the most ordinary day can become a memory. I'm thankful for the moments we've shared and excited for all the good ones still ahead. Here's another little piece of our story. 🫶🏻

You're Appreciated, Dora 💐

I hope you know that your presence matters. You don't have to do something extraordinary every day to be important to someone. Sometimes, simply being there is enough. Thank you for every moment you've shared with me, for every laugh, and for every little thing that made this friendship ours. I'm genuinely glad I got to know you.

Always Your Proton ⚛️

And finally, Dora... No matter how much I tease you, how much nonsense I talk, or how many times I annoy you 😭😂 Please remember that I genuinely appreciate you and the friendship we share. I'll keep cheering for you, celebrating your happiness, and wishing good things for you. Thank you for being my Dora. From your Proton, with a grateful heart. 💗

This letter isn't a request for anything. You don't have to find the perfect reply. I just wanted to make you smile and remind you that your friendship has made a difference in my life. Thank you for everything, Divya. I'm lucky to call you my best friend. 🫂💗
