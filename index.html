PAGE 01 · THE BEGINNING

To my Dora, Divya 🌷

I don't think a simple “thank you” could ever hold everything I want to say to you. Some people enter our lives quietly and become a place our hearts can rest. Somehow, you became that person for me. This little website is for every conversation, every laugh, every time you stayed, and every little thing you might not realise mattered.
With gratitude, your Proton 💌
PAGE 02 · ELECTRON TO PROTON

You changed something in me ⚛️

You always joke that you converted me from an electron into a proton — and honestly, that's such a Dora way to describe something meaningful. You brought more positivity into my world. You reminded me that even when my thoughts get too loud, someone can make things feel lighter. I'm still a work in progress, but I'm grateful for the little changes that began with you.
PAGE 03 · MY SAFE PLACE

You became my safe place 🫶

Thank you for being someone I can talk to without making every feeling sound perfect. Thank you for listening to my random thoughts, serious moments, and all the bakwaas in between. With you, I don't always have to pretend everything is fine. You made being understood feel a little less impossible. I'll always be thankful for the comfort and trust we've built.
PAGE 04 · THE LITTLE THINGS

It's the little things 🌼

It's not only the big moments I'll remember. It's the small check-ins, the silly jokes, the way an ordinary conversation can turn a day around, and the memories that looked simple when they happened. You may not notice every little thing you do, Dora, but some of those things have meant more to me than I can explain. Thank you for being you — without needing to do anything extraordinary.

PAGE 06 · MY FAVOURITE CHAOS

Never stop being Dora 😭

Thank you for the laughter, teasing, weird conversations, and the kind of friendship where even nonsense becomes a memory. You have this way of making ordinary things feel special without even trying. And yes, I will absolutely continue to irritate you — it's part of my job description now. 😌 You're stuck with your Proton, respectfully. 😂
PAGE 07 · WHAT I ADMIRE

I hope you see it too ✨

I hope you know that you matter beyond what you do for other people. You deserve kindness on cheerful days and on days when you don't have the energy to be cheerful at all. You don't have to fix everything or always have the right words. Your presence, honesty, and the person you are already mean a lot. I hope life gives you back some of the warmth you give to others.
PAGE 08 · WHEREVER LIFE GOES

Different roads, same gratitude 🌙

Life will keep changing. Our routines might change, our days might get busier, and there may be times when we don't talk as much as we'd like. But wherever life takes me, I'll always be grateful that I got to know you. I'll be cheering for you, wishing good things for you, and hoping you never forget how much you deserve them. And obviously, I'll still be there to irritate you. 😁
PAGE 09 · FROM YOUR PROTON

You don't have to earn your place 💗

You don't have to be useful, funny, strong, or available every second to be important to me. Friendship isn't a scorecard, and you never need to carry every conversation perfectly. I appreciate you for who you are — not because you always know what to say, but because you're Divya. My Dora. The friend who helped an electron feel a little more like a proton. Thank you for being part of my story.
PAGE 10 · THANK YOU FOR EVERYTHING

Thank you, Dora 🫂

If I could give you one thing, it would be the chance to see yourself through the grateful eyes of the people whose lives you've made brighter. Thank you for the memories, comfort, laughs, patience, and the little ways you helped me grow. Thank you for being a safe place without even knowing how much that meant. No matter where life takes me, I'll always be there to irritate you. 😁 Always cheering for you, your Proton ⚛️💗
P.S. You're not getting rid of me that easily, Dora. 😂
