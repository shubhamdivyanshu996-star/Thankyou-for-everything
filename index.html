Dear Dora, 💗

Dear Divya,

I don't think a simple thank-you could ever explain how grateful I am that you entered my life, but I still want to try.

Thank you for listening to my endless bakwaas, for being patient with me, for laughing with me, and for making ordinary conversations feel special.

Thank you for being a safe place in a world that sometimes feels too loud. For letting me talk, overthink, be weird, and be myself without always having to find the perfect words.

I know I can be complicated sometimes. I overthink, go quiet, and struggle to explain what's happening inside my head. But your friendship has reminded me how much a little understanding can mean.

And Dora, I hope you know that this friendship isn't only about what you've done for me. Your happiness matters to me, too. Your dreams, your difficult days, your little victories — I hope you always know that you deserve to be heard and appreciated.

You don't have to be strong every day. You don't always have to make everyone else smile. I hope you can be yourself around me, even when life feels difficult. You deserve the same warmth you give to the people around you.

Maybe life will get busy, and maybe our routines will change. But I'll always be thankful that our roads crossed and that I got to call you my best friend.

No matter where life takes us, I hope we keep sharing our nonsense, cheering for each other, and irritating each other for a very long time. 😭

Thank you for entering my life. Thank you for being my Dora. Thank you for helping this little electron feel a little more like a Proton.

I'm forever thankful for you, Divya. More than these words could ever explain. 💗

Always your Proton,
with love and gratitude. ♡
